Wi-Fi ALL DEVICES

On the same Wi-Fi? Enter the five letters on your laptop, then approve your phone there. Or scan its controller QR to pair instantly.

Code pairing uses local discovery. If your laptop cannot be found, scan its controller QR instead.

Open laptop pairing page ↗
Wi-Fi blocks the connection?

On your laptop, click Allow local connections. If that does not help, click Start offline Wi-Fi. Scan Join Wi-Fi, confirm joining on your phone, then scan Open controller. Stay connected when your phone says “No internet”.
